# Sunflower Microgreens: The Crunchy Snack (Not Just a Garnish)

Sunflower microgreens (sunflower shoots) are young sunflower plants harvested as greens — typically nutty, mild, and crunchy. They’re one of the easiest microgreens to eat as a **snack**, not only as a plate garnish. Fuel Foods packs them as **Sunnies Snacks** for people who want fresh crunch without a fried chip.

## Taste and feel

Sunflower shoots land differently from delicate leaf-only greens:

- **Nutty / slightly sweet / clean green** — mild enough for hesitant first-timers
- **Substantial stem crunch** — the snap is the product, not a side effect

## Storage for maximum crunch

Fridge. Dry. Don’t wash early.

## FAQ

### Can you eat sunflower microgreens raw?

Yes — preferred. Raw is where the crunch and nutty flavor show up. Cooking softens the snack job.

### What do sunflower shoots taste like?

Nutty, mildly sweet, clean green. Milder than peppery radish micros; more stem substance than delicate leaf mixes.

### Sunflower microgreens vs sunflower sprouts?

Microgreens are cut above the medium after true leaves begin — stem and leaves, not the root ball. Sprouts are typically the whole germinated seed eaten earlier and softer. Fuel sells micro sunflower shoots as Sunnies Snacks, not sprout jars.

### Filling enough to be a snack?

Practically: many people snack them solo; others pair with hummus, yogurt, or nuts. That’s preference and portion, not a satiety medical claim.

### Kids and sunflower shoots?

Mild and crunchy often works for curious eaters. Know your household’s preferences and allergies — this isn’t pediatric advice.

### Sunflower allergy note?
