# How to Use Microgreens: Creative Ways to Add Nutrition to Every Meal

Now that you know what microgreens are, let's explore the countless ways you can incorporate these nutritional powerhouses into your daily diet. Their versatility makes them perfect for any meal, from breakfast to dinner and everything in between.

## Storage and Handling

Before we dive into recipes, proper storage is essential for keeping your microgreens fresh:

### Storage Tips
- **Refrigeration**: Store at 35-40°F (2-4°C)
- **Moisture**: Keep slightly moist but not wet
- **Container**: Use breathable containers or bags with air holes
- **Shelf life**: Best used within 5-7 days of delivery
- **Don't wash until use**: Moisture accelerates spoilage

### Preparation
- Rinse gently under cool water just before eating
- Pat dry with paper towels or use a salad spinner
- Trim any remaining root mat if present
- Use immediately after washing for best texture

## Breakfast Ideas

### Morning Smoothie Boost
Add a handful of mild microgreens to your morning smoothie:
- **Sunflower microgreens**: Nutty flavor, high in protein
- **Pea shoots**: Sweet and mild
- **Broccoli microgreens**: Barely noticeable, maximum nutrition

**Recipe**: Blend 1 cup of sunflower microgreens with banana, mango, Greek yogurt, and almond milk for a protein-packed start.

### Elevated Eggs
Transform any egg dish:
- Top scrambled eggs with pea shoots and radish microgreens
- Add arugula microgreens to omelets
- Garnish avocado toast with mixed microgreens
- Fold microgreens into egg wraps or burritos

### Breakfast Bowls
Create Instagram-worthy breakfast bowls:
- Açai bowls topped with sunflower microgreens
- Yogurt parfaits with a microgreen garnish
- Oatmeal with a handful of mild microgreens stirred in

## Lunch Applications

### Sandwich and Wrap Upgrades
Replace or supplement lettuce with microgreens:
- **Pea shoots**: Perfect for chicken or turkey sandwiches
- **Arugula microgreens**: Add peppery kick to Italian subs
- **Mixed microgreens**: Enhance veggie wraps
- **Sunflower microgreens**: Great on grilled cheese

### Salad Enhancement
Use microgreens as a salad base or topping:
- Mix with baby greens for added texture
- Use spicy radish microgreens for a peppery kick
- Top Caesar salad with broccoli microgreens
- Create a simple microgreens salad with lemon vinaigrette

### Soup Topping
Add fresh flavor and nutrition:
- Garnish tomato soup with basil microgreens
- Top miso soup with radish microgreens
- Finish butternut squash soup with sunflower microgreens
- Add to pho or ramen just before serving

## Dinner Creations

### Protein Pairings
Microgreens complement all proteins:
- **Fish**: Top salmon with pea shoots or dill microgreens
- **Chicken**: Garnish grilled chicken with arugula microgreens
- **Steak**: Add radish microgreens for a peppery contrast
- **Tofu**: Pair with sesame ginger and sunflower microgreens

### Pasta Dishes
Fresh finish for hot pasta:
- Toss arugula microgreens into hot pasta (they'll wilt slightly)
- Top carbonara with pea shoots
- Garnish pesto pasta with basil microgreens
- Add to mac and cheese for nutrition boost

### Pizza Topping
Add after cooking for maximum freshness:
- Scatter arugula microgreens over Margherita pizza
- Top white pizza with pea shoots
- Add radish microgreens to BBQ chicken pizza
- Create a microgreens and prosciutto pizza

### Grain Bowls
Perfect for Buddha bowls and grain bowls:
- Quinoa bowls with roasted vegetables and mixed microgreens
- Rice bowls topped with protein and sunflower microgreens
- Farro bowls with a generous microgreens portion
- Poke bowls finished with radish microgreens

## Snacks and Appetizers

### Dips and Spreads
- Stir microgreens into hummus
- Top cheese boards with microgreens
- Mix into guacamole
- Garnish bruschetta

### Finger Foods
- Top deviled eggs with radish microgreens
- Add to spring rolls
- Garnish canapés
- Include in sushi rolls

## Beverages

Yes, you can drink your microgreens!

### Green Juices
- Juice with apples, celery, and cucumber
- Mix with lemon and ginger for a health shot
- Combine with pineapple and mint

### Cocktails and Mocktails
- Muddle pea shoots in mojitos
- Garnish Bloody Marys with mixed microgreens
- Add to green goddess smoothies
- Create microgreen-infused water

## Meal Prep Tips

### Weekly Integration
1. **Monday**: Prep smoothie bags with pre-portioned microgreens
2. **Tuesday**: Add to lunch salads
3. **Wednesday**: Top dinner proteins
4. **Thursday**: Mix into grain bowls
5. **Friday**: Create gourmet sandwiches
6. **Weekend**: Experiment with new recipes

### Portion Guidelines
- **Smoothies**: 1/2 - 1 cup
- **Sandwiches**: 1/4 - 1/2 cup
- **Salads**: 1-2 cups as base, 1/4 cup as topping
- **Garnishes**: 2-3 tablespoons
- **Cooked dishes**: 1/2 cup (add after cooking)

## Flavor Pairing Guide

### Mild Microgreens
- Sunflower, pea shoots
- Pair with: Any dish, especially good for beginners

### Spicy Microgreens
- Radish, arugula, mustard
- Pair with: Rich meats, creamy dishes, mild proteins

### Herb Microgreens
- Basil, cilantro, dill
- Pair with: Matching cuisines (Italian, Mexican, etc.)

### Earthy Microgreens
- Broccoli, kale, cabbage
- Pair with: Eggs, grains, Asian dishes

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Cooking them**: Microgreens lose nutrients when heated; add to hot dishes at the very end
2. **Overdressing**: Their delicate leaves can't handle heavy dressings
3. **Waiting too long**: Use within a week for best flavor and nutrition
4. **Skipping the wash**: Always rinse just before use
5. **Using only as garnish**: Don't be shy—use them as a main ingredient!
