# How to Store Microgreens So They Last All Week

Store microgreens in the refrigerator, as dry as practical, in their pack or a lined container. Don’t wash until you’re ready to eat. Fuel Foods packs are intended to keep in the fridge **up to 7 days depending on variety** when handled well. Harvest-to-door freshness helps; wet, warm, or sealed-swamp storage still ruins good greens.

**Moisture is the enemy.** Harvest-fresh still dies in a wet sealed tub.

## Step-by-step: door → fridge

1. **Refrigerate soon after delivery.** Don’t leave the pack on the counter while you unpack groceries. Cold slows wilting.
2. **Keep original packaging if it’s working.** If the greens look dry and perk, leave them alone. If moisture is pooling, move on to step 3.
3. **Line with a dry paper towel if moisture shows.** A clean container lined with a dry towel pulls condensation off stems. Replace the towel when it feels damp.
4. **Avoid condensation traps.** A warm pack sealed tight sweats. Let it chill first; don’t create a swamp.
5. **Optional: keep away from ripening fruit** if your fridge layout allows. Ethylene from fruit can speed soft greens along — useful when practical, not a religion.

| Step      | Do                                         | Don’t                          |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Arrival   | Fridge within the hour                     | Leave on the counter overnight |
| Moisture  | Dry paper towel; replace when wet          | Store dripping wet             |
| Wash      | Rinse right before eating                  | Wash the whole pack on day one |
| Container | Breathable or lightly vented if lined      | Seal wet greens airtight       |
| Placement | Cold shelf, not the door if it swings warm | Stack under heavy takeout      |

**Quotable:** Wash later. Store dry. Cold crunch beats slimy regret.

## Wash timing

Don’t wash early. Water is the start of the slime clock.

Rinse gently right before you eat — a quick cold rinse in a colander, then spin or pat dry if you’re building a salad. If a pack arrives looking dusty (rare with a harvest-to-door grower), use food-hygiene common sense: rinse, dry thoroughly, then return to the fridge lined with a fresh towel.

Washing “for later” is how apartment fridges invent wilt by Wednesday.

## How long do microgreens last?

**Fuel Foods guidance:** up to **7 days in the fridge by variety** when stored well. That’s the ops-locked window — not a lab study, not a guarantee for every fridge in Brooklyn.

- **Midweek:** eggs, bowls, sandwiches — the greens still look alive.
- **Later days:** egg scramble finish, quick pesto, mild smoothie handfuls — texture softens; flavor still works.

Harvest-to-door timing (~24–48 hours as marketed on [fuelfoods.store](https://fuelfoods.store)) means more of those usable days land in _your_ fridge, not a long wholesale chain. Phrase carefully: fresher start, not a miracle.

Variety matters. Sturdy sunflower shoots often hold crunch longer than delicate leaf mixes. Soft brassicas and mixed packs vary. “By variety” is the rule — don’t invent day-by-day charts without ops data.

## Past-prime signs (food quality, not medical advice)

- **Slimy / strong off smell / heavy mush** → discard. When in doubt, toss.
- **Slightly limp, smell fine** → smoothie, pesto, or cooked finish on eggs. The snack job is over; the flavor job isn’t.
- **Yellowing / drying** → use soon, or freeze _only_ for smoothies (salad crunch will not survive).

This is produce judgment, not a diagnosis. Trust your nose and eyes.

## FAQ

### Do you wash microgreens before storing?

No. Wash right before you eat. Early washing adds moisture; moisture shortens fridge life.

### How long do Fuel Foods microgreens last in the fridge?

Up to **7 days depending on variety** when stored cold and dry. Peak crunch is earlier in the window; later days still work for eggs, pesto, and smoothies.

### Can you freeze microgreens?

For smoothies, yes — texture will collapse. For salad crunch or desk snacks, no. Freeze only what you won’t finish fresh.

### Why did my pack wilt fast?

Usually warmth, moisture, or washing too early. Crowded NYC fridge doors and condensation traps are common. Move to a colder shelf, dry the pack, and replace wet towels.

### Airtight container — yes or no?

Dry greens in a lined container can work. Wet greens sealed airtight become a swamp. If you see condensation, vent or retowel — don’t trap the drip.

### Do sunflower shoots store differently?

Often they hold crunch longer than delicate leaf mixes, but still follow dry-fridge rules. Eat Sunnies while snap is the product; soft shoots move to eggs or blends.

### What if they’re limp but not spoiled?

Smell fine and not slimy? Use in eggs, pesto, or a smoothie. Don’t force a salad garnish job on limp greens.

### Does variety change shelf life?

Yes. “Up to 7 days by variety” is intentional. Sturdy shoots and mixed packs behave differently — store dry and use peak days for raw crunch.
